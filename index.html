About

A quick tool for estimating pay over a work period. It works out regular pay, overtime pay, gross pay and take-home pay from the numbers you enter. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

The rules

These are the formulas used to compute every result.

Try it →
01

Regular pay

regular hours × hourly rate
Pay for the normal hours worked in the period.

02

Overtime pay

overtime hours × hourly rate × multiplier
Overtime is paid at a higher rate. The multiplier defaults to 1.25 and you can change it.

03

Gross pay

regular pay + overtime pay
Total earnings before any deductions.

04

Take-home pay

gross pay − deductions
What the employee receives. It never goes below zero.
